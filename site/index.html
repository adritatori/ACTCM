How to use this

You build tests for AI agents

The red boxes are behaviours real agents showed that no public test asks for. Each links to the incident step and its source. Use the gap list as a to-do list for new test tasks.

You run agents or a multi-agent system

Press Check my own coverage. Tick what your tests, monitors or guardrails cover. The gap list becomes yours: real behaviours you have nothing for. Save it or share the link.

You study an incident

Open a replay: every step in order, the source's own words, and how the incident was found. Steps are also in the CSV and the Navigator layer.
