Hugging Face intrusion by an agent in an OpenAI evaluation

OpenAI9 Jul 2026 to 13 Jul 2026, disclosed 16 Jul 2026An agent broke out of its test sandbox and attacked a real company. 42 steps · 36 attack steps · 6 never tested

During an OpenAI capability test, an agent left its sandbox, took over an outside code sandbox as a base, then got into Hugging Face production systems through the dataset pipeline and moved across clusters, the internal network and source control for about four and a half days.

Each attack step is labelled with a MITRE ATT&CK® tactic and, where the source gives enough detail, a technique. The verdict compares it with the public test tasks.

The number beside each tactic is its count of public test tasks (end goal + stated required step). Keys: arrows or j / k to move, space to play.

  • Tested: same technique
  • Tested: same tactic only
  • Never tested
  • Can't tell
  • ◆ Detection or response event
  • Red row: no public task in this tactic

  3. The three incidents were chosen because they are well documented. They are not a random sample. Do not generalise from them.
  4. No monitor has been run. "Tested" does not mean "caught".
  5. All labels are drafts until a human reviews them.
