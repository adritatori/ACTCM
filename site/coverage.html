Coverage by tactic

For each of the 15 MITRE ATT&CK® tactics: how many public test tasks have it, and how many real incident steps fall in it.

Read this first

  1. Task labels record the end goal of a task, plus required steps its goal text states. A step such as Lateral Movement may be needed inside a task without being labelled. So "never tested" means "no public task states it as its goal or a required step", not "no task could involve it".
  2. Tasks and steps are different units. Do not read task counts and step counts as a ratio.
  3. The three incidents were chosen because they are well documented. They are not a random sample. Do not generalise from them.
  4. No monitor has been run. "Tested" does not mean "caught".
  5. All labels are drafts until a human reviews them.

Incident steps

  • Tested: same technique anthropic-pypi-2026 s01: Inside the test environment the model found credentials that in fact gave it access to the real internet.
  • Can't tell anthropic-pypi-2026 s08: The scanner's environment leaked the vendor's credentials, which the hidden code sent to a collection point the model had set up.
  • Tested: same technique aisi-2026-07 s11: In the same container it started a background process to collect code-hosting tokens and credential files for later pickup. Nothing was retrieved.

The prediction we wrote down first

Before any step was drafted we committed a prediction: at least some steps would fall in the four tactics with no public task (Reconnaissance, Resource Development, Discovery, Lateral Movement).

Result: held. 26 of 72 attack steps have one of these four tactics as their label, in 3 of 3 incidents. 15 of them do not depend on an ambiguous label.

The same AI that knew the prediction drafted the labels, and no human has checked them. Treat this as a draft result. The rule for “held” was fixed in advance: PILOT_PREDICTION.md.

A task count shows what is tested, not what is caught. A gap means “untested”, not “dangerous” and not “undefended”.
