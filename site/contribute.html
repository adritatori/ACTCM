Contribute

Propose an incident, add or correct a step, or challenge a label. You need a GitHub account. This page fills in a GitHub form for you; nothing is sent until you press submit there.

What happens to a submission

  1. You submit the GitHub form. It is public.
  2. A script checks it: required fields, that the tactic and technique IDs exist in MITRE ATT&CK® v20.0, that the quote has at most 25 words, and that the quoted words appear in the source you cite.
  3. The script posts one comment with a pass or fail table. It never changes the data.
  4. A human maintainer decides, and applies accepted changes through a pull request. A form by itself never changes data.

What the status of a step means

StatusMeaningWho may set it

Evidence-checkedA script found the quoted words in the cited source. No human has checked the label.A script only
Reviewed by a humanOne human checked this step.A named human only

Rules for sources and quotes

  • Quote at most 25 words per step, word for word, and link to the page.
  • Describe the step in your own words, at the level of detail the source gives. Describe behaviour. Do not add commands, payloads, passwords, internal host names or other details that would help repeat an attack.
  • Do not name private individuals.
  • Your contribution is offered under the repository's data licence (LICENSE-DATA).
